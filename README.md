# Longbox: a Marvel, DC & manga timeline

An interactive timeline of Marvel and DC comics and manga from 1935 to today: first appearances, series launches, landmark stories, crossover events and their tie-ins, and the industry history that ties them together.

## Running it

No build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder (for example `python3 -m http.server`) and visit it. It also works on GitHub Pages as-is.

## What you can do

- **Marvel / DC / Manga / All** switches between each one alone and everything together. Manga has its own eras and groups its series by the magazine they ran in.
- **Marvel / DC**: switches between each publisher alone and both side by side. Intercompany crossovers always show in their own lane.
- **Timeline** view: scroll through time, Ctrl/⌘ + scroll (or the slider) to zoom. Zoomed out you see the whole history as colored marks; zoomed in you get covers. **Whole history** fits all of it on screen, and the minimap at the bottom shows how busy each year was. Click or drag it to jump.
- **Series** view: every series as one row on the same time axis, with every issue at its release date. Group by franchise or show everything in release order. Zoom in for covers. Pick an event to light up every tie-in across all series; tap a series name to see its whole run.
- **Covers** view: every entry as a cover, grouped by decade and year.
- **Creator spotlights**: creators big enough to get their own lane, like Frank Frazetta, with their whole career including work outside Marvel and DC.
- **Characters**: pick a hero or villain to follow just their issues, including the events they were part of.
- **Events**: the purple bars. Open one for its reading order, core series and tie-ins.
- **Big screen** (button in the toolbar, or press F): hides the menus, makes covers half again as large and goes full screen. Press play (or space) to auto-scroll slowly through history, and pick Slow, Medium or Fast. The controls fade when the mouse rests. Esc leaves.
- **Flip through covers**: click the cover in any issue's panel (or "Flip through covers" on a series) to see covers full screen. Step through the whole run with the arrows, the arrow keys, a swipe, or the slider; "Details" opens the issue you stopped on.
- **Print covers**: lay out the covers you're looking at (a series, an event's reading order, a hero's appearances, a creator's issues, or the timeline's key issues) in a grid, then print it or save it as a PDF. Pick covers per row and a year range.
- **Search** matches titles, characters, writers, artists and notes.
- **Edit details / Add issue**: fill in writers, artists, cover image links and notes. Edits are saved in your browser; **Your edits** lets you copy them out so they can be added to the data files.

## The data

| File | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `data/marvel.js` | Marvel issues |
| `data/dc.js` | DC issues |
| `data/events.js` | Crossover events (shown as bars), Marvel, DC and intercompany |
| `data/manga.js` | About 150 key manga series, Japanese industry history, and the moments manga and American comics crossed paths |
| `data/spotlights.js` | Creator spotlights (their own timeline lane and career panel) |
| `data/crossovers.js` | Intercompany crossovers, shared industry history, and hero/villain tags |
| `data/series.js` | Full runs of 2,576 series (46,000+ issues), every Batman and Spider-Man appearance, and 65 event reading orders from Metron, generated by `tools/fetch-series.mjs` |
| `data/covers.js` | Cover image links from Metron, generated by `tools/fetch-covers.mjs` |

Each issue looks like this:

```js
{ date: "1962-08", title: "Amazing Fantasy", issue: "15", type: "first",
  characters: ["Spider-Man"], writers: ["Stan Lee"], artists: ["Steve Ditko"],
  summary: "…", event: "", cover: "" }
```

- `date` is the **cover date** (`YYYY-MM`, or `YYYY`). Cover dates ran two or three months ahead of the on-sale date.
- `type` is `first`, `launch`, `key`, `tiein` or `milestone`. Events live in `events.js`.
- `event` links a tie-in to an event id from `events.js`.
- `cover` is an optional image URL. Without one, the app draws a cover in the publisher's colors.

The dataset is a curated starting point of about 280 entries and 66 events, not a complete index of every issue.

## Cover images

Covers come from the free [Metron](https://metron.cloud) comic database. With a Metron account:

```sh
METRON_USERNAME=you METRON_PASSWORD=secret node tools/fetch-covers.mjs
```

In a Claude cloud session, the login is stored as a network secret for `metron.cloud` instead, and Node needs to be told to use the session's proxy:

```sh
NODE_USE_ENV_PROXY=1 NODE_EXTRA_CA_CERTS=/root/.ccr/ca-bundle.crt node tools/fetch-covers.mjs
```

This looks up each issue and writes the links to `data/covers.js`. It takes about 10 minutes because of Metron's rate limit, and it's safe to stop and re-run. A `cover` set directly on an issue overrides the fetched one.

## Full series and event reading orders

`tools/fetch-series.mjs` builds `data/series.js` from Metron: the complete issue list of every series on the timeline and of every series that tied into an event, plus each event's reading order. It makes about 1,700 requests (an hour at Metron's rate limit), caches progress in `tools/metron-cache.json.gz`, and can be stopped and resumed. Run it the same way as the cover import.

## Manga covers

Manga cover images come from [AniList](https://anilist.co). The browser looks them up the first time Longbox opens (about 15 seconds in the background) and remembers them, so it only asks once.
